## How to choose

Pick for the **buyer who will browse the category**, not for how the product is built. A scraper bought by sales
teams belongs in Marketing, not DevTool.

| Product type (Apify actors) | Recommended 3 | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Lead lists for sales, BD or agencies (exhibitors, business directories, company data) | Marketing, Productivity, SaaS | Buyers browse Marketing; the promise is saved time; it's a hosted tool |
| E-commerce or marketplace data (prices, products, sellers) | Ecommerce, Analytics Tool, SaaS | Sellers and analysts browse Ecommerce |
| Job postings or recruiter data | Hiring, Productivity, SaaS | Recruiters browse Hiring |
| SEO, SERP or keyword data | SEO Tools, SEO Analytics tool, Marketing | Exact match exists |
| Social media data | Social Media Management, Marketing, Analytics Tool | Social managers browse there |
| Finance, crypto or market data | Finance (or Crypto), Analytics Tool, SaaS | |
| News or content monitoring | News, Analytics Tool, Productivity | |
| Agent or MCP utilities, developer-facing APIs | DevTool, AI (only if it really uses AI), SaaS | Buyer is a developer |
| Email finding or enrichment | Marketing, Email Marketing, Productivity | Say plainly it finds emails and doesn't send them |

Rules of thumb:
- **AI** only when the product really uses an AI model. A false AI label costs trust with exactly the people who
  click it.
- **Email Marketing** implies a tool that sends emails. Use it only as a third pick for email *finding*, and say
  "finds, doesn't send" in the description.
- **SaaS** is a safe filler for any hosted Apify actor (nothing to install, runs in the browser).
- **Chrome Extension** and **Mobile App** describe the delivery form. Pick them only when the product really is one.
